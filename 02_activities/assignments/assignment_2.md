    Visualization 1: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/rafael.centeno/viz/2021F1Season/2021F1SeasonInCharts

    This Tableau dashboard offers a comprehensive overview of the 2021 Formula 1 season, highlighting key statistics and trends across various races and drivers.​ I would consider this an effective data visualization. The visualization also uses suitable chart types, such as line charts for tracking points over time and bar charts for comparing race results.     Visualization 2: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/vizgrowth/viz/F1Circuits2024/Dashboard13 
     
    This Tableau dashboard aims to present information about the 2024 Formula 1 circuits.​ Although this visualization is nice to look at, I would consider it a poor visualization mainly because of the lack of contextual information. The dashboard displays circuit layouts without providing additional context at first glance. When you click into each circuit image, it displays the circuit names and location, but it doesn't not provide any other relevant information. In addition to knowing the order of the F1 Grand Prix  for the 2024 season, a user may also want to know the start time of each race, the number of laps, and whether the race is a sprint or not, or other significant information about the circuit. This lack of information makes it difficult for users to derive meaningful insights from the visualization.​ The presentation of numerous circuit layouts in a compact space also leads to a cluttered appearance that might overwhelm the viewer who maybe looking for a particular bit of information. Finally,​ the dashboard lacks interactive features that could enhance user engagement, such as filters to explore specific circuits in detail.
